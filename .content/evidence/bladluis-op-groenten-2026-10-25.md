# Evidence register — 2026-10-25
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/biodiversity/aphids — aphids widespread, support biodiversity and natural enemies; control only when necessary; avoid pesticides.
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/prevention-protection/controlling-pests-and-diseases-without-chemicals — physically wash aphid colonies off, favor hoverflies/predators, avoid untested DIY detergents and oils which can injure plants and beneficial life.
UC IPM https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/aphids/ — insects under leaf, honeydew/ants, predatory insects, water spray, care with products and heat-sensitive leaves.
UConn https://ipm.cahnr.uconn.edu/scouting-guidelines-and-biological-control-options-for-vegetable-bedding-plants-and-transplants/ — monitor vegetable tips and leaf undersides, sticky honeydew, cast skins, curled new leaves.
Rutgers https://extension.rutgers.edu/fs230 — excess nitrogen encourages aphids, predators, nonchemical management.
Claim limitations: symptoms alone are not diagnostic, soap/oil home brews discouraged, regulated product permission/label must be verified locally.

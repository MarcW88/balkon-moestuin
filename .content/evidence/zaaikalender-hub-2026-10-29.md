# Evidence — Zaaikalender hub
Research on 29 October 2026: RHS vegetable seeds sowing https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/seeds-sowing confirms sowing timing varies by crop; December/January mainly for planning; February indoor/protected salads; successive sowing for lettuce, radish, carrots, chard. RHS beginners seed sowing https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/beginners-guide/vegetable-basics/soil-preparation/seed-sowing-techniques distinguishes indoor sowing, outdoor sowing and protected sowing, warns about soil temperature, light and hardening off. RHS September jobs https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/grow-your-own/in-month/september-jobs confirms overwintering spinach/winter lettuce/rocket. RHS garlic https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/garlic/grow-your-own confirms planting cloves in autumn rather than seed sowing. Not firsthand trials; UK seasonality not blindly transferred to Netherlands.

# Evidence — 2026-10-28
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/fruit/raspberries/grow-your-own (web fetched): summer types fruit on last year's canes, normally 1.8m, yield generally second summer; autumn types fruit on current-year canes often 1.2–1.5m; self-fertile; compact container varieties, minimum 40 cm wide x 30cm deep, single cane in peat-free/loam-based mix, stake; after harvesting remove fruited summer canes and keep young replacements; February prune all old autumn canes; protect long-cane summer planting from automatic short cutting. RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/education-learning/school-gardening/resources/getting-started/container-gardening: compact Ruby Beauty and Glen Fyne, renew perennial pot compost. RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/fruit/fruit-trees/containers: pot fruit needs watering, repotting/compost refreshing. RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/watering: small fruit including raspberries needs steady moisture at flowering to harvest.

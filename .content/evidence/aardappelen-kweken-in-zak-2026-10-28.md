# Evidence, 2026-10-28
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/potatoes/grow-your-own: first earlies late March, second earlies early/mid April, maincrops mid April to early May; 30cm wide/deep container, one seed potato per 30cm pot diameter, peat-free compost, incrementally earth up to cover tubers; first earlies June/July, second July/August, maincrop later; seed potatoes and optional 4–6-week chitting; empty bags with drainage. RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/education-learning/school-gardening/resources/food-growing/how-to-grow-potatoes-in-containers: use healthy seed potatoes, compost, draining container, earthing up and harvest; RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/container-gardening/growing-plants-in-containers for container watering and drainage. Belgian public health authority https://www.health.belgium.be/fr/professionnels/entreprises/produits-consommation/alimentation/securite-alimentaire/contaminants-chimiques/nitrates-autres-substances-vegetaux: green potatoes and glycoalkaloid risk; discard green tubers. No hands-on trials.

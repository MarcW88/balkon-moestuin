# Sources vérifiées
RHS How to water containers https://www.rhs.org.uk/container-gardening/how-to-water-containers : check moisture a third down; water early morning/early evening, slowly at soil level; stop before/as drain first drips; no fixed watering schedule, larger/deeper pots retain moisture longer, terracotta dries quickly, winter saucers should be removed. RHS maintenance https://www.rhs.org.uk/container-gardening/maintenance : check regularly in warm and windy weather, even after rain; drought wilting harms growth. RHS growing plants in containers https://www.rhs.org.uk/container-gardening/growing-plants-in-containers : drainage holes essential, waterlogging causes root problems. RHS water use research https://www.rhs.org.uk/science/gardening-in-a-changing-world/water-use-in-gardens/watering-containers : excessive watering not automatically beneficial; experimental ornamental plant figures should not be copied as vegetable-dose recommendations. Original source University of Minnesota Extension retained; not presented as local proof. No own watering experiments.

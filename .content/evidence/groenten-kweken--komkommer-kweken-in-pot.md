# Evidence — komkommer in pot
Verified on 2026-10-28 against https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/cucumbers/grow-your-own
- Outside cucumber varieties suitable for warm sheltered sunny sites; greenhouse cultivars are not automatically suitable outdoors: VERIFIED.
- Minimum pot size of approximately 30 cm wide and deep: VERIFIED, minimum not universal optimum.
- Indoor sowing in late April, around 21 C for germination, individual pots and avoid root disturbance: VERIFIED.
- Plant outdoors after hardening from early June when temperature reliably above roughly 12 C: VERIFIED, weather dependent.
- Regular moisture, liquid feed in pots and potash-rich feed when flowering: VERIFIED.
- Outdoor cultivars typically keep male flowers for pollination; greenhouse varieties have different rules: VERIFIED, cultivar-specific caveat required.
- Outdoor pruning after seven leaves is described by RHS: VERIFIED; not universal for all cultivars.
- High/windy balconies and load-bearing capacity cannot be assessed without building-specific data: LIMITATION.
No first-hand trial, pricing or yields claimed.

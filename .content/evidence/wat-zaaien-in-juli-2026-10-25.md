# Evidence — 2026-10-25
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/grow-your-own/in-month/july-jobs — direct sow turnips, pak choi, tatsoi, mizuna, chicory, Chinese cabbage, autumn salads, carrots; late French and runner beans only in south England (so weaker fit Netherlands).
Intratuin https://www.intratuin.nl/inspiratie/moestuinkalender-juli — July snijbiet, rucola, veldsla, spinazie, sla, winterpostelein, kale, carrots and beet by mid-July; pak choi and Chinese cabbage after July 15 to reduce bolting.
Moestuinplanner https://www.moestuinplanner.nl/zaaikalender/juli/ — crop-specific late summer dates, autumn leafy varieties and succession.
Limitations: hot July sowing for spinach/field salad may fail; describe late-summer start instead of promising reliable July success. No own trials.

Moestuinplanner https://www.moestuinplanner.nl/zaaikalender/november/ — weeuwensla, winter snijsla, early carrots under glass; autumn garlic planting, seasonal harvest and winter preparation.

Crop and location caveats: autumn sowing not a guaranteed November harvest; reduced light and frost matter. No own trials.

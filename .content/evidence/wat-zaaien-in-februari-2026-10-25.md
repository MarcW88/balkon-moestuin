# Research evidence — 2026-10-25
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/grow-your-own/in-month/february-jobs : chit early potatoes, tomatoes/chillies/cucumber/aubergine mid-Feb for greenhouse growing, early outdoors only in mild sites on light soils; otherwise March/April.
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/seeds-sowing : sow under cover from February with lettuces/salad crops; plant sowing technique differs by crop.
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/education-learning/school-gardening/resources/food-growing/month-by-month-guide-to-food-growing-spring-term : broad beans, peas, radishes can be sown in Feb; prepare containers and protection.
Intratuin https://www.intratuin.nl/inspiratie/moestuinkalender-februari : early sowing depends on temperatures and cover, winter remains cold and dark.
Editorial caution: outdoor date and exact germination success contingent on site, protection and cultivar. No own trials.

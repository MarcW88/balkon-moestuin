# Evidence — wat heb je nodig — 2026-10-29
- RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/container-gardening/growing-plants-in-containers : containers require drainage holes, appropriate growing medium, regular checking, more water/feeding in restricted root volume; large containers dry more slowly; overpotting can cause waterlogging; peat-free media available.
- RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/containers : suitable crops include salads, radish, herbs, tomatoes; choose container according to species.
- RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/container-gardening/how-to-plant-up-a-container : drainage holes are essential; a gravel base is not a substitute for functional drainage.
- Milieu Centraal https://www.milieucentraal.nl/huis-en-tuin/tuin/stappenplan-groen-balkon/ : take into account weights of wet planters, people, water, furniture. Consultation with VvE, landlord or engineer as necessary. Its generic reference kg/m² is not approval for a specific structure.

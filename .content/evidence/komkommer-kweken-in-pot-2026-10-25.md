# Verified reference register — 2026-10-25
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/cucumbers/grow-your-own
- Outdoor or protected cultivars have different conditions. Sunny, warm and sheltered outside.
- Containers >=30cm wide AND deep for one plant, peat-free multipurpose medium.
- For outdoor growing: sow late April indoors one seed 1–2cm deep around 21°C; harden off; plant out from early June when local temperature >12°C reliably, conditional on weather.
- Climbing supports occupy less floor space. Stable supports needed.
- Regular water for containers, frequent checks during heat, fertilize 10–14 days; high potash feed at flowering.
- Outdoor kinds need male/female flower pollination; don't remove male flowers. Most all-female greenhouse kinds must avoid pollination depending on cultivar.
- Outdoor pinch growing tip after seven leaves; harvest minis roughly 8cm, cultivar-dependent.

- Drainage holes essential, containers dry rapidly; feeding/watering necessary.

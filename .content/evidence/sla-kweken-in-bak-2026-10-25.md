# Evidence register — 2026-10-25
- RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/lettuce/grow-your-own : containers at least ~30cm width, sow ~1cm depth, loose-leaf spacing ~15cm versus hearting ~30cm, spring-summer sowing, hot/dry conditions can induce bolting, need regular moisture; harvest leaf lettuce about 4-6 weeks favorable conditions.
- RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/cut-come-again-salads : picking outer leaves while growing point remains, repeat small batches rather than one crop, containers suitable.
- RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/containers : root space, drainage, water management.
- Unverified: actual yields, local weather timing, effectiveness of specific equipment. No hands-on trial.

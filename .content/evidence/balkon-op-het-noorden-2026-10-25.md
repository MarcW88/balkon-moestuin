# Verified sources, 2026-10-25
1. RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/garden-design/shade-gardening — chard, kale, leaf salads, lettuce, peas, spinach relatively tolerant of light shade. Deep shade != light shade.
2. RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/containers — vegetable growing in containers possible if suitable container chosen; drying out, root space, nutrient issues.
3. Cornell extension https://cals.cornell.edu/school-integrative-plant-science/school-sections/horticulture-section/outreach-and-extension/pandemic-vegetable-gardening/pandemic-vegetable-gardening-2021-archive/no-yard-grow-vegetables-and-more-containers — leafy crops may manage with less light; full shade is poor for vegetables; fruiting crops need much sun.
4. Maryland extension https://extension.umd.edu/resource/growing-vegetables-containers (updated 2026-03-23) — orientation and containers.
Unverified: real balcony yield, cultivar-specific production, exact exposure of reader's home. No fabricated testing.

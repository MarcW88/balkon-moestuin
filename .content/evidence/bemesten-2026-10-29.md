# Evidence — Fertilization review 2026-10-29
Sources explicitly checked via web:
RHS How to feed plants https://www.rhs.org.uk/garden-jobs/nutrition-feeding-plants — containers commonly benefit after approximately six weeks; active growing season about fortnightly for fruit and vegetables, with product/plant exceptions; minimize unnecessary fertilizer.
RHS How to grow tomatoes https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/tomatoes/grow-your-own — organic high-K liquid feed every 10–14 days once first fruits swell, according to directions.
RHS Fertilisers https://www.rhs.org.uk/garden-jobs/fertilisers — soluble liquid vs controlled-release, follow product label for method/timing/dose.
University of Minnesota Extension https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/fertilizing-and-watering-container-plants — startup feeding may begin 2–6 weeks after potting depending on growing mix and growth; excess salts damaging; not a universal calendar.
RHS How plants absorb nutrients https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/understanding-plants/how-plants-absorb-nutrients — N/P/K functions and micronutrients.
No first-hand fertilizer trial or product comparison. UK/US contextual guidance not represented as precise Dutch local trial evidence.

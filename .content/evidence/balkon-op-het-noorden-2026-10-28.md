# Evidence — 2026-10-28
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/garden-design/shade-gardening (web checked): beetroot, chard, kale, leaf salads, lettuce, spinach and others tolerate light shade; direct sun in midsummer helps. RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/containers: well-draining containers suitable for leafy crops. University of Maine Extension https://extension.umaine.edu/gardening/2023/06/16/what-vegetables-and-fruits-will-grow-in-an-area-that-only-gets-three-to-five-hours-of-sunlight-a-day/ specifically 3–5h as lower viable light zone, leafy greens more likely, fruiting crops require much more direct sun. Iowa State Extension https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/faq/which-vegetables-will-grow-partial-shade supports ~3–4 hours for greens. Original Cornell https://cals.cornell.edu/school-integrative-plant-science/school-sections/horticulture-section/outreach-and-extension/pandemic-vegetable-gardening/pandemic-vegetable-gardening-2021-archive/no-yard-grow-vegetables-and-more-containers retained as existing link.

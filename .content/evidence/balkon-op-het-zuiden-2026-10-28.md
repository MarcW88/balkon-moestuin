# Evidence — 2026-10-28
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/container-gardening/how-to-water-containers (web fetched): southern and western containers tend to dry faster than north/east; terracotta loses moisture faster; metal overheats; larger deeper pots retain moisture; water well when needed and self-watering as a holiday option.
University of Maryland Extension https://extension.umd.edu/resource/growing-vegetables-containers-and-salad-tables (updated Sep 10 2026; fetched): southern/western exposure warmest, 6–8 hours sun for fruiting vegetables vs 3–5 for cool-season greens, heat sinks (brick/concrete/metal), drainage hole importance, gravel bottom does not improve drainage, container size and heavy wet pot risks, differences in water demand basil/lettuce vs thyme/rosemary; moisture testing before watering. Original RHS vegetable container and tomato source URLs retained. No first-hand experiments.

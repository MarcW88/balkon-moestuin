# Evidence — Balkon inrichten
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/container-gardening/growing-plants-in-containers : drainage holes essential, larger pots reduce rapid drying but may be heavier, wind and winter waterlogging are issues, container plants need maintenance. RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/containers : vegetables can thrive in containers if crop/container fit, smaller containers risk limited root room/moisture/nutrients. These are horticultural sources only. Balcony-load engineering claims intentionally limited: no universal capacity assigned, no assumption balcony railing can take hanging pots; recommend building specifications/owner/VvE/structural engineer for uncertainty. No first-hand practical trials.

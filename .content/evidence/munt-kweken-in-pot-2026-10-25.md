# Evidence — checked 2026-10-25
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/herbs/mint/grow-your-own
Mint perennial; commonly hardy; full sun or partial shade; rhizomes spread, own container preferable; divide and repot every few years; stem cuttings root easily in water; sow spring, press seeds lightly onto seed compost at ~20 C, germination ~2 weeks; regular young shoot harvest spring-autumn; water during dry spells; mint rust possible.
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/herbs/containers
Mint likes moist fertile growing medium but avoid soggy pots; overwinter may die back; rich compost and rejuvenation as needed.
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/grow-your-own/containers/april-edible-container-idea/june-edible-container-idea?lnk=1
Mint needs a separate pot from Mediterranean herbs because roots spread, moisture requirements differ.

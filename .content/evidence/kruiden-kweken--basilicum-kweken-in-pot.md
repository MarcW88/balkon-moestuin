# Evidence — Basilicum
Checked 2026-10-28.
- RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/herbs/basil/grow-your-own — tender sun/heat-loving herb, protect after last frost; germination around 20 C; succession sowings, container at least 20 cm across/deep, remove flowers and harvest shoots: VERIFIED.
- RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/herbs/containers — sheltered site and caution with dense supermarket plants grown under glass: VERIFIED.
- University of Minnesota Extension https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/yard-and-garden-problems/growing-basil — roughly 6-8 hours bright light and well-drained soil: VERIFIED as guideline, not a promise.
- RHS notes supermarket pots are intended mainly for short-term harvest; indoors preferred due to biosecurity. Copy now cautions against direct outdoor placement.
- No own cultivation experiment, guarantee of summer harvest or product test claimed.

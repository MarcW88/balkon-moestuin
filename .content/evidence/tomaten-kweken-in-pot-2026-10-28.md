RHS Tomatoes in containers https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/grow-your-own/containers/veg-on-walls/may-edible-container-idea — sunny sheltered place, pot around 30 cm for compact cherry, frost-free nights, feeding and summer fruit.
RHS Blossom end rot https://www.rhs.org.uk/problems/blossom-end-rot — physiological calcium problem tied to poor uptake, particularly moisture stress; no automatic lime recommendation.
RHS watering https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/watering — inconsistent moisture and blossom end rot.

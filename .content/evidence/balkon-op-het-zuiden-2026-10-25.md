# Evidence — 2026-10-25
CONFIRMED: South and west exposures are generally sunnier/warmer, but actual shading must be checked (University of Maryland Extension, https://extension.umd.edu/resource/growing-vegetables-containers-and-salad-tables).
CONFIRMED: Warm-season crops need substantial direct sun; cool-season leafy plants need less (same source).
CONFIRMED: Containers dry faster than in-ground soils; southern positions often dry faster than northern ones; check soil moisture and prefer watering in cooler hours (https://www.rhs.org.uk/container-gardening/how-to-water-containers).
CONFIRMED: Tomatoes outdoors need sunny sheltered conditions and steady moisture (https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/tomatoes/grow-your-own).
CONFIRMED: Container crops include tomatoes, peppers, radish, leaves and herbs; choose suitable container (https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/containers).
UNKNOWN: exact harvest quantities, local balcony wind and structural load, exact frost-free outdoor date, product performance. No first-hand tests.

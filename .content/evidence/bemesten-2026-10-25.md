# Evidence — 2026-10-25
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/garden-jobs/nutrition-feeding-plants : containers often need feeding after around six weeks, during active growth; general liquid feed every two weeks for vegetable containers as example, lower fertilizer use possible; acid-loving blueberry uses appropriate feed.
UMN Extension https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/fertilizing-and-watering-container-plants : start after approx 2-6 weeks depending on potting mix and growth; soluble vs slow-release, NPK, leaf/fruit differences; follow manufacturer dosing; risk of excess fertilizer.
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/tomatoes/grow-your-own : once first fruits swell, high-potassium organic liquid feed around every 10-14 days according to label.
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/containers : contained roots have limited nutrients, watering can leach nutrients.

RHS advises certified seed potatoes; early types are often practical in containers. Start with a large container with drainage, partly fill with compost, plant seed potatoes with spacing, gradually add compost around shoots (earthing up), keep moisture consistent, harvest based on variety and growth. Green potatoes and potato fruits should not be eaten. Chitting may give a head start, but is optional. Local weather and frost determine outdoor timing.

# Evidence — 2026-10-28
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/fruit/blueberries/grow-your-own read on 2026-10-28: pH ideally 4.5–5.5, peat-free ericaceous medium, young plants >=30cm pot, mature 45–50cm, compact varieties Bluetta / Sunshine Blue, full sun and partial shade, most partially self-fertile but two different flowering-compatible cultivars improve harvest, prefer rainwater, first two years little pruning, then dormant pruning up to a quarter old shoots, berries ripen progressively, prevent wildlife entanglement when protecting from birds, winter containers at risk.
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/education-learning/school-gardening/resources/food-growing/growing-fruit-in-containers: feed ericaceous liquid around monthly, repot blueberry container every two years.
University of Minnesota extension https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/growing-blueberries-in-the-home-garden was original attribution retained; claims primarily cross-checked against RHS. No personal grow trials.

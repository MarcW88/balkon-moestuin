# Evidence — 2026-10-28
University of Maine Cooperative Extension https://extension.umaine.edu/gardening/2023/06/16/what-vegetables-and-fruits-will-grow-in-an-area-that-only-gets-three-to-five-hours-of-sunlight-a-day/: 3–5 hours minimum boundary, leafy lettuce/spinach/chard/greens possible, root crop radish uncertain, fruiting crops need much more sunlight. Iowa State University Extension https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/faq/which-vegetables-will-grow-partial-shade: lettuce/spinach/collards/swiss chard/kale in 3–4 hours; additional partial-shade candidates. RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/garden-design/shade-gardening: light shade tolerable for leafy greens. RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/garden-inspiration/grow-your-own/crops-for-shade: partial shade may slow bolting in lettuce/spinach. Iowa State https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/how-to/growing-edible-plants-shady-areas: no productive vegetables in full shade. No original tests.

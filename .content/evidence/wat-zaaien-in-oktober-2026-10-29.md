# Evidence — verified 29 October 2026
RHS October jobs https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/grow-your-own/in-month/october-jobs : sow overwintering broad beans/peas only in mild areas; plant garlic and winter onion sets; clear harvest debris. RHS autumn calendar https://www.rhs.org.uk/education-learning/school-gardening/resources/food-growing/month-by-month-guide-to-food-growing-autumn-term : October winter lettuce, broad beans, garlic cloves, onion sets and peas; differentiates sow versus plant. RHS Garlic https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/garlic/grow-your-own : autumn to early winter planting; pots at least 25 cm deep; cloves approx 15 cm apart and tip 2.5 cm below surface; good drainage, risk in wet winter soil. RHS broad beans https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/broad-beans/grow-your-own and RHS feature https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/grow-your-own/features/sowing-broad-beans : dwarf varieties can grow in containers, windy or wet winters favor spring sowing alternative.

# Evidence — fetched October 29, 2026
RHS March jobs https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/grow-your-own/in-month/march-jobs: March sowings in mild regions with suitable soil include beans, carrots, beetroot, lettuces, radishes, peas, spinach and other greens; indoor peppers, tomatoes, aubergines. RHS tomatoes https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/tomatoes/grow-your-own: greenhouse sow late Feb to mid March; outdoors sow late March to early April. RHS cucumbers https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/cucumbers/grow-your-own: outdoors sow indoors late April; heated greenhouse earlier only. RHS What to sow in March https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/grow-your-own/features/what-to-sow-in-march: carrots spinach beetroot salad leaves radish peas and beans; avoid hasty second sowings.

# Sources checked 2026-10-25
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/herbs/basil/grow-your-own
- Indoors sow March–July at ~20°C; outside sow/plant when warm, night temperatures consistently >10°C; grow sunny and sheltered.
- Pot at least ~20cm wide and deep, peat-free compost, 10cm between plants.
- Even moisture, morning watering, do not waterlog.
- Balanced feed can help leafy growth; high-potassium tomato fertilizer not ideal.
- Harvest shoot tips, cut flower stems to maintain leaves; bring indoors when cold.
RHS https://www.rhs.org.uk/herbs/containers
- Containers possible; supermarket herbs often grown crowded and stressed, so acclimation needed.
UMN Extension https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/yard-and-garden-problems/growing-basil
- Six to eight hours good bright light; prune above leaf pair for new shoots; basil tender frost-sensitive.

# Evidence — aardappelen kweken in zak — 2026-10-30

RHS How to grow potatoes: https://www.rhs.org.uk/vegetables/potatoes/grow-your-own

- Early varieties suit containers because they mature sooner.
- Use containers at least 30 cm wide and deep, with drainage; plant one seed potato per 30 cm pot diameter.
- Gradually add compost as shoots grow, keeping new tubers shielded from light.
- Water regularly, protect new shoots from frost and discard green tubers.

RHS potatoes in containers: https://www.rhs.org.uk/education-learning/school-gardening/resources/food-growing/how-to-grow-potatoes-in-containers

- Select suitable seed potatoes; pre-sprouting is optional.
- Keep the bag draining and harvest without damaging tubers.
- Potato berries and green tubers must not be eaten.
